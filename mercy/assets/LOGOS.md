# Technology marks

The report embeds selected SVG marks from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), distributed under its [CC0 license](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md). Downloaded 2026-11-26 from the project's `develop/icons/` directory: python.svg, fastapi.svg, html5.svg, javascript.svg, huggingface.svg, nvidia.svg, ffmpeg.svg, sqlite.svg and react.svg.

`technology-logos.json` contains the embedded data URLs. These are static, bundled assets; the generated report does not request images from a CDN. Marks identify technologies and resource hosts, not an endorsement or proof of configuration. Trademark rights remain with their owners. Unknown marks use a text monogram.
